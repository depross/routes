Railway & wood

Hop fields, the old railway bed, a chapel hamlet and the wood.

10,6 kmabout 2 h 25Poperinge

Good to know

Mostly quiet asphalt lanes with two unpaved stretches: the old railway bed in the north and the paths through Helleketelbos at the end. After rain the forest paths are muddy; the railway is fine in any weather.

Water and a snack in the bag: there is no café on the line until Boskafé Boule Carré, which sits 400 m south of the wood on Stoppelweg (Friday to Monday from noon, Sunday from 11:30).

More

The railway is line 69A, Poperinge to Hazebrouck across the French border, opened in 1870 and closed to passengers in the 1950s. The bed is now a long green corridor used by cyclists and walkers; the stretch here runs between hop fields and small woods.

Helleketelbos, at the far end, is a small replanted wood on a low rise managed by Natuur en Bos: oaks, sheep, a half-timbered cottage at the entrance and views to the French hills from its south edge.

The walk

Clockwise. Distances are from the front door.

0,0 km

Out of the door

Down the gravel lane to Kleine Dasdreef, then left, up the dreef to Helleketelweg and west. At the Karel Grimminckweg junction stay right and take Duivinnestraat north through the hop fields.

Clockwise: north to the old railway first, the wood near the end.

2,8 km

Left onto the old railway bed

The Poperinge to Hazebrouck line, closed decades ago and now a car-free green path. Dead straight, trees either side, west to Sint-Jan-ter-Biezen.

Nothing but bikes and walkers from here to the hamlet.

3,5 km

Sint-Jan-ter-Biezen

A hamlet with a chapel on the border of hop country. Leave the railway here and turn south-west on Kapellestraat.

Halfway. A bench by the chapel.

5,0 km

South to Helleketelweg

Kapellestraat becomes Karel Grimminckweg and drops back to the Helleketelweg junction you passed earlier. Cross it and keep straight on, south, with the wood ahead.

7,0 km

Wood on your left

The forest edge now runs alongside the lane. Keep following the asphalt along the edge of the wood, down to the bottom corner.

7,4 km

Leave the asphalt

At the corner where the lane bends away, take the track into Helleketelbos. Packed earth, roots, a boardwalk where it stays wet.

White-and-red GR 5A marks and knooppunt signs along the way.

7,8 km

Through the wood

East along the path through the heart of the wood, then it swings north and climbs gently out towards the lane. Oaks, sheep in the open parts, roe deer if you are lucky.

Boskafé Boule Carré is a few hundred metres south, on Stoppelweg, if the legs want a stop.

Helleketelbos, VijfgemetenstraatOpen in Maps

8,1 km

Back onto a lane

The path comes out on Vijfgemetenstraat at the top corner of the wood. Turn left; the lane heads north-east.

8,5 km

Right onto Kleine Casseldreef

A long, straight lane between the fields, pointing almost exactly at home.

A bit under a kilometre of straight line.

9,4 km

Left, and up the dreef

Where Kleine Casseldreef meets Kleine Dasdreef, turn left up the dreef, then left again into the gravel lane for the last stretch to the door.

10,6 km

Home

Boots off. 10,6 km in all, 0,9 km of it unpaved.

About 2 h 25 at a walking pace, longer if you stop.
